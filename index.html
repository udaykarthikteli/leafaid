The process

From leaf to remedy in four steps.

No lab equipment, no waiting for a specialist. Point your camera, and Leaf Aid does the rest — while showing its work at every step.

01 — Capture

Photograph the leaf

Snap a photo in natural light, or upload one from your gallery. Works on damaged, spotted or partially eaten leaves.

02 — Scan

The model examines it

A convolutional vision model segments the leaf, isolates lesions, textures and insect damage in under three seconds.

03 — Explain

See the reasoning

A heatmap overlay shows exactly which regions drove the diagnosis, alongside a plain-language explanation.

04 — Act

Get a treatment plan

Receive dosed treatment options, organic alternatives and a prevention checklist tailored to your crop.

Everything you need

One tool, from first symptom to full recovery.

Leaf Aid isn't just a classifier — it's a full diagnosis-to-treatment workflow built for growers, students and agronomists.

Explainable AI, not a black box

Every diagnosis ships with a Grad-CAM-style heatmap, bounding boxes on affected regions, and a written rationale — so you can verify the model instead of just trusting it.

AI chatbot support

Ask follow-up questions in plain language — "is this contagious?", "what's a safe organic spray?" — and get instant, grounded answers.

One-tap image upload

Drag, drop or snap a photo. Leaf Aid handles blurry shots, mixed lighting and multiple leaves in one frame.

Treatment & prevention

Dosed, crop-specific treatment plans plus a prevention checklist so the disease doesn't come back next season.

A dashboard that remembers everything

Every scan is saved with its confidence score, severity and treatment history — so you can track how a field is recovering over an entire season.
